Los Rios Transfer Workshop Series

These workshops are meant to assist and inform students who are intending on transferring for 4-year degrees. Students can attend workshops facilitated by various universities that cover different aspects of transferring. We encourage any student to attend and learn more about the transfer process!

Note: To help us improve this resource, students may be requested to provide feedback on this page.
